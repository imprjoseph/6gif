About the Summit

Shaping the next era of global connectivity

6G Summit Taipei brings together government leaders, industry pioneers, academia and innovators to exchange perspectives on the future of 6G.

The summit explores vision, key technologies, spectrum, standards and emerging applications—accelerating collaboration for a smarter and more sustainable future.

Two-Day Program

Agenda Highlights

DAY 1 · SEPTEMBER 23, 2026

  • Opening Ceremony & Keynote
  • 6G Vision, Technologies & Standardization
  • Innovative 6G Applications
  • Industry Collaboration & Ecosystem

DAY 2 · SEPTEMBER 24, 2026

  • Policy, Spectrum & Regulatory Outlook
  • Security, Trust & Network Resilience
  • Global Cooperation & Business Opportunities
  • Closing Keynote & Summit Wrap-up

The agenda is preliminary and subject to change.
