About the Summit

About 2026 6G Summit Taipei

As the global telecommunications industry stands at the threshold of a new technological era, the 2026 6G Summit Taipei convenes leading voices from academia, industry, and government to chart the path toward an AI-Native 6G future. Taking place on September 23–24, 2026, at the Taipei International Convention Center, the summit examines how artificial intelligence will reshape the sixth generation of wireless communications. The event will spotlight cutting-edge developments and challenges poised to transform key sectors in the AI-native 6G era.

  • Session 1: AI-Driven 6G Era in 2030s
  • Session 2: 6G Key Drivers: Spectrum, ISAC, NTN, and Low Altitude Economy
  • Session 3: 6G Standards and Technology Development
  • Session 4: 6G Industry Development

Day One explores AI-driven connectivity’s impact on society and industry, and Day Two turns to standards development and industrial deployment, addressing global 6G direction and native AI integration—culminating in a showcase of 6G pilot projects from Taiwan and beyond.

Distinguished speakers from EU, USA, Japan, Korean and local Taiwan will tackle the technical, regulatory, and commercial challenges of bringing 6G from vision to reality, reinforcing Taiwan’s position at the forefront of next-generation communications innovation.
